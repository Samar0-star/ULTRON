Structural Physics of the SARS-CoV-2 RBD–ACE2 Interface (6M0J): Site-Specific Interaction Mapping

1. Atomic Architecture of the Binding Interface

The receptor-binding domain (RBD)–ACE2 complex (PDB 6M0J) presents a binding interface whose energetic burden is concentrated in a small set of RBD residues. The internal hypothesis ledger (Hypothesis 9a07c250, refuted; Hypothesis e4ee349d, refuted) identifies the operative structural unit as the ACE2 490–505 loop pocket formed by Asp30, Asp38, and Lys353, into which the RBD receptor-binding motif inserts. Within this pocket, two RBD positions dominate the mutational landscape examined here: Q498 and N501, with a recorded Cβ–Cβ distance of 5.32 Å. Note that this adjacency call derives from the 6M0J contact map under a heavy-atom contact criterion; the Cβ metric and the heavy-atom criterion are not strictly equivalent, and the adjacency designation should be treated as contact-map-derived rather than a direct geometric measurement. The interface as scored by the DMS cross-analysis comprises n = 20 interface residues in 6M0J and n = 399 interface mutations out of 4,221 total single mutations (Hypothesis d80bdf24, refuted), i.e., approximately 9.5% of the scanned mutational space carries interface status.

The physical logic of the pocket is electrostatic-hydrophobic complementarity. Lys353 of ACE2 forms a salt-bridge-capable contact with the RBD; Asp30 and Asp38 define the acidic rim of the pocket. Q498 and N501 sit at the loop apex that inserts into this pocket; their side chains jointly determine the loop's polar/hydrophobic surface presented to ACE2. Any mutation at these positions perturbs (i) direct contact energy with D30/D38/K353, (ii) the local loop conformation (backbone φ/ψ at 498–501), and (iii) indirectly, the positioning of neighboring interface residues. This three-term decomposition — direct contact, conformational, indirect — is the mechanistic basis for expecting non-additivity, and it is the mechanism that Hypotheses 9a07c250 and e4ee349d formalized and that the internal refutation pipeline rejected in their unqualified forms.

import numpy as np

def epistasis_deviation(e_wt, e_i, e_j, e_ij, n_perm=1000, seed=0):
    """
    eps_ij = ln(e_ij) - ln(e_i) - ln(e_j) + ln(e_wt)   [e4ee349d definition]
    Permutation null: resample eps from product of independent marginals;
    two-sided p-value at n >= 1000 permutations.
    Inputs: enrichments (linear scale, WT-normalized), clipped to >= 1e-6.
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    floor = 1e-6
    lw = np.log(max(e_wt, floor))
    li = np.log(max(e_i, floor))
    lj = np.log(max(e_j, floor))
    lij = np.log(max(e_ij, floor))
    eps = lij - li - lj + lw
    pool = np.array([li, lj])
    null = np.empty(n_perm)
    for k in range(n_perm):
        a, b = rng.choice(pool, size=2, replace=True)
        null[k] = lij - a - b + lw
    p = float(np.mean(np.abs(null - np.mean(null)) >= abs(eps - np.mean(null))))
    return eps, p
