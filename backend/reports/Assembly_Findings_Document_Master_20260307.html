  • The provenance schema may become overly intricate, reducing practical usability. Mitigation: enforce a minimal viable provenance granularity and iterative refinement with mandatory sampling audits.

7) Concluding synthesis and forward-looking implications

By instrumenting a rigorous, falsifiable pathway from data through explicit inference boundaries to dynamic, versioned claims registries, this program anchors C0 in a transparent, auditable, and testable methodological ecosystem. The three proposed experiments—provenance enforcement (Experiment I), cross-domain replication and sensitivity analysis (Experiment II), and counterfactual falsification (Experiment III)—collectively address the most consequential epistemic gaps: (i) formal provenance of implicit inferences, (ii) cross-domain corroboration under explicit regime boundaries, and (iii) principled falsification through interventional data synthesis. The boundary conditions and decision rules specified herein are designed to prevent both overconfidence in weak signals and stagnation in the presence of genuine but complex causal structure. Realizing these objectives will yield a definitional advance in the discipline: a reproducible, transparent, and scalable framework for moving implicit inferences into explicit, testable scientific claims.
